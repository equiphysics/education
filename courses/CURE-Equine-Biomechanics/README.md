# Equine Biomechanics CURE (BME 492, Spring 2026)

Syllabus for a course-based undergraduate research experience (CURE) in biomedical engineering at the University of Arizona. Student teams run a real study: does music change a horse's gait, heart rate, or heart-rate variability? They help design the experiment, collect gait and heart data with horses under standard conditions, manage and document the data, analyze it, and report the results.

**Audience and level:** upper-level undergraduates in engineering or science. One to three credits, lab-only. Horse experience helps but is not required. Safety training is.

## Files

- `BME492_Equine_Biomechanics_CURE_Syllabus_Sp2026.docx`: course information, goals and ABET mapping, grading, proposal requirements, weekly expectations, safety rules, and a 15-week milestone timeline.

## How to use it

Adapt the structure for your own CURE. The data in `labs/Horses-and-Music` and the notebooks in `analysis_templates` show what the students work with.

Campus phone numbers were removed from this public copy. Add your own institution's support contacts and accessibility statement.
