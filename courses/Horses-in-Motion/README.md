# Horses in Motion: A History of the Biomechanics of Movement

Syllabus for a six-week, in-person course taught by Laura Miller in spring 2025 at the University of Arizona's Al Marah Equine Center. Two hours a week, with the horses close by.

**Audience and level:** adult learners and curious horse people. No math or science background needed.

The six weeks: the ancient partnership between horses and humans; how selective breeding shaped breeds and their movement; the story of the Al Marah Arabian; the biomechanics of the walk, trot, canter, and gallop; modern tools (high-speed video, inertial sensors) for measuring gait and lameness; and what horse movement teaches robotics and horse-human interaction. Each week has a reading from a short list of books and open-access articles.

## Files

- `Horses in Motion Syllabus.docx`: the syllabus with weekly summaries and the reading list

## How to use it

Take the weekly plan and the reading list as a starting point for your own course, reading group, or barn talk. The labs in this repo (`labs/`) fit weeks 4 and 5.
