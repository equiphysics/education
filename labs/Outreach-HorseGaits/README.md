# Horse Gait Lab

A 60-minute hands-on activity on horse gaits as coupled oscillators. Students clap out the walk, trot, pace, canter, and gallop, then turn footfall timing into a phase, a number between 0 and 1, with the interactive web app at https://equiphysics.github.io/web-apps.

**Audience and level:** high school and up. No horse experience and no equations needed. The math is counting beats, fractions of a stride, and reading a phase wheel. Written for a high-school class in 2026; it also works at outreach events and barn days.

## Files

- `Horse_Gait_Lab.pptx`: the slide deck
- `Horse_Gait_Lab_Worksheet.docx`: the student worksheet. The QR code on the worksheet points to https://equiphysics.github.io/web-apps.
- `Horse_Gait_Lab_Teacher_Guide.docx`: setup, timing, and talking points
- `Horse_Gait_Lab_Answer_Key.docx`: answers for the worksheet
- `Canter_animated.gif`: the canter animation used on the slides

## How to run

1. Open the web app at https://equiphysics.github.io/web-apps on a laptop, tablet, or phone. Nothing to install. Students can scan the QR code on the worksheet to open it on their own phones.
2. Print one worksheet per student. Read the teacher guide once before class.
3. Present from the deck and have students work through the worksheet as you go. Keep the answer key for yourself.
