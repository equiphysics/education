# Horses and Math (ages 6 to 12)

Two-hour hands-on lesson for ages 6 to 12: count gait beats with a metronome and hobby horses, then compare kid, horse, and animal heart rates on a printable chart.

**Audience and level:** kids aged 6 to 12, with one adult leading and one helper. The math is counting beats, counting for 10 seconds and multiplying by 6, putting numbers in order, and subtraction. The kids need no horse experience. You need one calm horse and a handler who can lunge it at the walk and trot.

## Files

- `Horses and Math Lesson Plan.docx`: the plan, editable. Page 1 is the setup checklist and the two-hour timeline, with a math hook for each block. Page 2 is the Heartbeat Chart to print big.
- `Horses and Math Lesson Plan.pdf`: the same plan, ready to print.

## What you need

- A mechanical metronome (the weight slides to 60, 100, and 144)
- A stopwatch
- Four hobby horses
- The Heartbeat Chart printed once, large, on a clipboard, with markers
- A Polar Equine strap and the phone app, or a stethoscope as a backup
- Snacks and water for the two breaks

## How to run

Read the plan once. Set up before the kids arrive (the checklist is on page 1). Then follow the timeline: four feet versus two feet at the fence, hobby-horse gaits, stepping to the metronome, a break, clapping the real horse's hoofbeats, a break, heartbeats with the Polar strap, a jump test, and a wrap-up where every kid shows one gait.
